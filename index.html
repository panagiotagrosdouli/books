A note from the writer

Curiosity
is the first
plot twist.

I write stories about mystery, memory, love, technology, and the unknown. My books move between science fiction, crime, romance, and speculative fantasy, but they all begin with the same question: what happens when ordinary people face something impossible?

I enjoy creating atmospheric worlds, complex characters, hidden truths, and stories that stay with the reader after the final chapter. From artificial intelligence and forgotten cities to unsolved murders and unfinished love stories, each book is an invitation to step into a different world.

“What happens when ordinary people face something impossible?”

I would love for you to find your next favorite world here.

ABOUT EMMA

Who I am
and why I write.

I’m Emma. I write mysteries and romances, strange futures and impossible places, and stories shaped by myth and history.

WHY I WRITE

I like starting with a question that has no easy answer: what if the future left a memory? What if a city could change the rules of time? I write to follow the people who find themselves inside those questions—and see what they choose next.

This page is my bookshelf, gathered in one place. I’m glad you’re here.
